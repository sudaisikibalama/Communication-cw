Introduction

The District Digital Learning and Teacher Resource Hub is an online platform designed to support students,teachers,parents and school administrators in accessing educational information and learning materials.

Sections of the platform are;

Welcome message.
About the portal.
Mission and vision.
Educational objectives.
District announcements.

MISSION:
To improve the quality of education through the provision of accessible digital learning resources and teacher support services.

VISION:
To create a digitally connected educational community where every student and teacher can access quality educational resources.

OBJECTIVES:
To promote digital learning in secondary schools.
To provide access to revision materials.
To support teacher's professional development.
